How long does road freight from Romania to Greece take?

As a guide, a full truck reaches Thessaloniki in about 2 days and Athens in 2 to 3 days, depending on the loading point and the delivery window. With regular daily departures, you rarely wait for a truck. Groupage takes a little longer because of consolidation, and the quote confirms the transit time for your addresses.

Which vehicles do you use between Romania and Greece?

On Greece we use standard 13.6 m semi-trailers for FTL, with up to 24 t or 33 Euro pallets. Groupage is loaded on pallets, in shared trailers that leave daily on the same lane. For project cargo we choose the vehicle according to the dimensions and weight of each load.

What kind of goods do you carry between Romania and Greece?

Palletised general cargo for every industry: manufacturing, automotive, wood and furniture, FMCG, agri-food and construction, plus project cargo. Building materials, wood and furniture, packaged food and consumer goods usually head to Greece; packaged food, packaging and industrial goods usually come back.

Which parts of Greece do you serve?

We deliver door to door across mainland Greece: Central Macedonia (Thessaloniki, Serres, Kilkis), Eastern Macedonia (Drama), Thessaly (Larissa, Trikala), Central Greece (Lamia), Epirus (Ioannina), Attica (Athens and the Aspropyrgos industrial area) and the Peloponnese (Corinth, Tripoli).

Which documents are needed for freight between Romania and Greece?

The goods travel with a CMR consignment note and the usual commercial documents, an invoice or delivery note. Romania, Bulgaria and Greece are all in the EU and Schengen, so goods in free circulation are not cleared through customs on the way. Where it applies, the transport is RO e-Transport compliant.

How is the price for a load to Greece calculated?

The price starts from the loading and delivery addresses, the type of transport (FTL, groupage or project cargo), the number of pallets or the weight, and the loading date. With daily departures, it is easy to find a truck for your date. The rate in the quote stays the rate, with nothing added after delivery.
