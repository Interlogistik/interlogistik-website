07FAQ

Before you ask.

Short answers to what transport and purchasing managers ask us first.

How long does transit take?

It depends on the distance, the service and the loading and delivery windows. Your quote includes an estimated transit time for your specific lane, and once the truck is moving, the tracking link shows the live ETA.

Is my cargo insured?

Yes. Every shipment travels under CMR insurance. If something does go wrong, we manage the claim for you, from the first report to the final settlement.

Do you subcontract to other forwarders?

No. We work directly with a fixed group of partner carriers. Your load is not resold on a freight exchange or passed down a chain of intermediaries.

How does GPS tracking work?

Every truck carrying your freight is on GPS. You receive a tracking link for your shipment and can check its position and ETA at any time, without calling for updates.

How quickly do I get a quote?

Within one working day. Send the loading and delivery places, the date and what you are moving. The price we quote is the price you pay, and there is no obligation.
