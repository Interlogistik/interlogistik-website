01The lane

Both directions, one standard.

Germany is Romania’s largest trading partner, and freight on this lane runs heavy in both directions. We plan it as one corridor: same carriers, same tracking, same commitments, whichever way the load is going.

Romania → Germany

From Alba Iulia and anywhere in Romania to any address in Germany: Bavaria and Baden-Württemberg in the south, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia, and on to Berlin and the north. Typical loads include components and finished goods from Romanian plants, furniture and packaged food.

Germany → Romania

From German suppliers to Romanian factories, warehouses and building sites: machinery and spare parts, raw materials, automotive components and retail stock.

Route

Through Hungary and Austria. The whole route runs inside the EU and the Schengen area, so goods in free circulation need no customs clearance.

How long does a full truck from Romania to Germany take?

As a guide, 2 to 4 days, depending on where in Germany it is going and on the loading and delivery windows. Your quote confirms the transit time for your exact addresses.

Do you also bring freight from Germany to Romania?

Yes. The lane runs in both directions with the same partner carriers, the same GPS tracking and the same commitments.

Do you deliver anywhere in Germany?

Yes, to any loading or delivery address in Germany. Send us the postcodes and we price the exact lane.
