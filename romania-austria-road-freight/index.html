01The lane

Both directions, one standard.

Austria is one of the closest Western European markets to Romania. On a short lane, what matters most is that the truck is on time and that you know where it is. That is how we run it.

Romania → Austria

From Romania to Vienna, Lower and Upper Austria, Styria, Salzburg and the industrial west: components, finished goods, wood products and furniture, food.

Austria → Romania

From Austrian suppliers to Romanian sites: steel and metal products, machinery, building materials and consumer goods.

Route

Through Hungary, entering Austria in the east. EU and Schengen all the way: no customs clearance for goods in free circulation.

Indicative transit

FTL
usually 1 to 2 days to eastern Austria, about 2 to 3 days to the west
Groupage
a little longer, depending on consolidation
Project cargo
planned load by load

Indicative only. The exact transit time depends on the loading and delivery points and on the service, and is confirmed in your quote.

How fast is a full truck from Romania to Vienna?

As a guide, 1 to 2 days, depending on the loading point in Romania and the delivery window in Vienna. The quote confirms the transit time for your addresses.

Can I send just a few pallets to Austria?

Yes, by groupage. Your pallets share a trailer with other loads on the same lane and you pay for the space they use.

What do you need from me for a quote from Austria to Romania?

The loading and delivery places, the loading date, the number of pallets or the weight, and what the goods are. You get a price within one working day.
